Put logo files, font files and any design exports in this folder alongside this file.

## Principles

Two or three lines on how the brand should look and feel, and what it should never look like.

## Colours

Each colour with its hex code and its job (main, accent, background, text). Say which colours lead and which are used sparingly, roughly in what proportion.

## Contrast

Which text and background pairs are safe to use and which aren't. Body text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1.

## Fonts

Which font for headings and which for body text, with weights and sizes. Which free font to use if the brand font isn't available.

## Logo

Which file to use where, the space to leave around it, the smallest size, and what never to do with it.

## Imagery

The style of photos, illustrations, icons or video: subjects, lighting, framing, people. What never appears. Where images come from (own shoots, stock, AI) and whether any of those are ruled out.

## Layout

Margins, spacing, alignment and how much text goes on an image.

## Formats

The sizes used on each channel, such as 1080 × 1350 for an Instagram post, and anything that changes per format.
