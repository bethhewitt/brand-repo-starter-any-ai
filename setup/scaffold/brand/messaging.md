## Pillars

The 3 (or at most 4) things the brand delivers, with no overlap between them. For each: a short name, 2 to 4 sentences that start with the audience's problem and then bring in the brand, and the proof behind it from `knowledge/proof.md`.

## What makes us different

Up to 5 reasons we deliver this better than the alternatives: things no competitor is saying that this brand can truthfully say. Each with a headline, a short paragraph and the evidence from `knowledge/proof.md`. None of them can be on the "What everyone says" list in `knowledge/competitors.md`.

## By persona

For each persona in `knowledge/audience.md`: a one-line lead message, and what each pillar means to them. If you could swap the persona's name and the line still works, it isn't specific enough. Leave this out if there are no personas.

## Objections and answers

For each thing under "What holds them back" in `knowledge/audience.md`: the objection in the audience's words, and how content answers it, with proof where there is some.

## Lines we can use

5 to 10 short lines for ads, social posts, emails and subject lines, each tagged with the pillar it comes from.

## Claims we can make

Each one with its proof from `knowledge/proof.md`.

## Claims we never make

Including anything ruled out by law or industry rules, such as health, financial or environmental claims.
