# Mission and vision

_Not filled in yet. Ask your AI to set up your brand by following `setup/brand-starter.md`._

## Mission

What the brand does, for whom, and why, today. One or two sentences, in the present tense.

**What it rules out:** one or two things the brand won't do because of it.

## Vision

The future the brand is working towards if the mission succeeds. Ambitious, but specific enough to say no to things.

## What we believe

3 to 5 convictions behind the mission. Write each one as something the brand does ("We let guests finish the story"), not an adjective ("Authentic"), with a line on what it looks like in practice.
