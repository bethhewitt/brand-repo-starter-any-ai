# Audience

Mark each point **(observed)** if it comes from real data such as analytics, reviews, surveys or sales, or **(assumed)** if it's the team's belief.

## In one line

Who the brand is for: the people who get the most from it and who matter most to it.

## Main audience

- **Who they are:** specific enough to target an ad. Age, life stage and location, or industry, company size and role for business buyers.
- **Why they matter:** what they're worth to the brand, and what they get from it.
- **Their world:** a short paragraph that describes their problem back to them, so they'd nod reading it.
- **What they're trying to get done:** the practical job, how they want to feel, and how they want to be seen.
- **What they do today instead:** alternatives, workarounds or doing nothing, and why those fall short.
- **What sends them looking:** the moments that make them look for something like this.
- **What holds them back:** what stops them choosing the brand, or staying.
- **Words they use:** real phrases from reviews, comments or messages, quoted.

## Other audiences

Up to two more, each with the same details in brief. Leave this out if the brand has one audience.

## Personas

1 to 3 people within the audiences above, only where they care about meaningfully different things (a buyer and a user, say). For each:

- **Name and one-line description**
- **Day to day:** what they actually do
- **Say in the decision:** decides, influences or just uses
- **What they care about:** what would make them look or feel good
- **What tips them:** the specific moment that would make them join or switch
