## The market

The 3 to 5 changes in the category, platforms or audience behaviour that affect what content should do. Each with one fact, where it came from and when, and what it means for this brand.

## Who we compete with

Whatever the audience would choose instead, not only brands like this one. For each: the name, website, and type:

- **Direct:** same offering, same audience
- **Indirect:** a different offering that does the same job
- **Do it yourself:** a manual or home-made alternative
- **Doing nothing:** sticking with what they have

## How they describe themselves

For each direct competitor: their headline, who they say they're for, the three things they lead with, and the proof they show. Quote their own words, and say where they came from.

## What everyone says

Claims and phrases most competitors use. Content that leans on these sounds like everyone else. How this brand is different goes in `brand/messaging.md`.

## How we talk about competitors

Whether content ever names a competitor, compares directly or responds to one, and how.
