## Pillars

The 3 (or at most 4) things the brand delivers, with no overlap between them. For each: a short name, 2 to 4 sentences that start with the audience's problem and then bring in the brand, and the proof behind it.

## What makes us different

Up to 5 reasons we deliver this better than the alternatives. Each with a headline, a short paragraph and the evidence.

## By persona

For each persona in `audience.md`: a one-line lead message, and what each pillar means to them. If you could swap the persona's name and the line still works, it isn't specific enough. Leave this out if there are no personas.
